Retail Metrics Interpretation Guide
How is Gross Revenue distinguished from Net Profit?

Gross Revenue represents total sales inflow. Net Profit represents actual retained earnings remaining after deducting cost of goods sold (COGS), inventory purchasing, store maintenance, and operational overhead.

What does a 35% Net Margin mean in operational practice?

A 35% margin indicates that for every ₹100 in consumer sales, approximately ₹35 is preserved as clean business net profit, reflecting healthy pricing power and cost discipline.

Why is Customer Churn Rate critical for store longevity?

Customer Churn measures the percentage of buyers who discontinue purchasing over time. Low churn (e.g. 2.1%) ensures reliable repeat revenue without requiring excessive acquisition ad spend.

How does AI Demand Forecasting optimize working capital?

High-confidence forecasting predicts required SKU volume in advance, preventing capital from being trapped in dead inventory while completely avoiding costly out-of-stock situations.
